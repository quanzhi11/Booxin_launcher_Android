Mesa 3D Graphics Library
https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa

BooxinGlues / BooxinZink ships a prebuilt Mesa OSMesa binary (libOSMesa_25.so)
with the Zink Gallium driver enabled at runtime (GALLIUM_DRIVER=zink).
